4 Contribute to the global model optional

Contributing improves the model for everyone without disclosing your measurements. What leaves your browser is listed in full below, and you can inspect it before sending.

What is sent

  • What you type in the form below: your name, laboratory, material, measurement method.
  • The number of rows and the range (min–max) of each cutting parameter.
  • Summary sums over your data — D'D, D'y, y'y, n — which let us refit the model on the pooled data exactly, without moving any measurement.
  • The coefficients fitted on your machine, and the two scores above.

What is never sent

  • Your measurement table. No row, no individual value, at any point.

One honest caveat: those summary sums are not anonymous. On very few rows they reveal a lot about the dataset — with a single row they would give it away entirely. This is why contributions need at least 5 rows, and why we say so here rather than claiming a guarantee we cannot make.

Required, because methods disagree: XRD and hole drilling differ by tens of MPa on the same part. Pooling them unknowingly would average two conventions.

The key keeps the global model from being altered by anyone passing by. Ask us if you do not have one.
